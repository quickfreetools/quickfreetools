About this tool

This free image compressor reduces file size while preserving visual quality, directly in your browser — no software or sign-up required.

  • Quality slider: Lower values produce smaller files but reduce image clarity. 70–85% is a good balance for most photos.
  • WebP format: Usually produces the smallest file size while keeping good quality — recommended for most uses.
  • Privacy: Your image is processed locally and never uploaded to a server.

Ideal for speeding up websites, reducing email attachment size, or saving storage space.
