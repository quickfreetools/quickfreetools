Common Passport Photo Dimensions

Many applications use sizes such as 2 × 2 inches or 35 × 45 mm. However, requirements depend on the issuing authority and document type. These presets are provided for convenient resizing and do not guarantee acceptance.

Private Browser-Based Photo Resizing

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a 2 × 2 inch passport photo?

Yes. Select the 600 × 600 pixel preset, which corresponds to 2 × 2 inches at 300 pixels per inch.

Can I resize photos to 35 × 45 mm?

Yes. The tool includes an approximate 413 × 531 pixel preset for 35 × 45 mm at 300 pixels per inch.

Will my photo be accepted for a passport or visa?

Acceptance is not guaranteed. Always check the official requirements for background, face size, head position, lighting and image format.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser.
