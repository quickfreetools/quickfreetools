Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Upload multiple images and arrange them in the order you want. The converter creates one PDF containing your images.

Does this JPG to PDF converter support PNG?

Yes. You can convert JPG, PNG and WebP images into a PDF document.

Is this JPG to PDF converter free?

Yes. The converter is free to use and does not require an account or add a watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image conversion happens locally in your browser. The PDF library is loaded from a CDN, but your images are not uploaded for processing.

Can I reduce the PDF file size?

You can select a lower image quality setting. This may reduce the PDF file size, although the result depends on the source images.

Does the converter work on mobile?

Yes. You can upload images, reorder them with the arrow buttons and create a PDF using a compatible mobile browser.
