About this tool

This free word counter helps writers, students and professionals quickly check text length for essays, articles, social media posts and more.

  • Words: Total word count based on spacing.
  • Characters: Includes spaces and punctuation.
  • Sentences: Counted by detecting sentence-ending punctuation.
  • Reading time: Estimated at an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, stored, or shared.
