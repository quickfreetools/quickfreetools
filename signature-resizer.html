How to Resize a Signature Online

  1. Upload your signature image.
  2. Drag to crop the area you want to keep.
  3. Select a preset or enter custom width and height.
  4. Set your maximum file size in KB.
  5. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP.
  6. Click Resize Signature and download the result.

Free Signature Resizer for Online Applications

Many online forms require signatures with specific pixel dimensions and file-size limits. QuickFreeTools lets you crop and resize a signature image without installing software.

All processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your signature is not sent to our servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize my signature to 300 × 100 pixels?

Yes. Select the 300 × 100 preset or enter your preferred dimensions manually.

Can I reduce a signature below 50KB?

The tool attempts to meet your selected file-size limit. Some images and formats may not reach extremely small targets.

Does the tool remove signature backgrounds?

No. Transparent pixels can be preserved when supported, but the tool does not automatically remove white or photographed backgrounds.

Is my signature uploaded?

No. Processing happens locally in your browser.
