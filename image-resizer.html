About this tool

This free image resizer lets you change the width and height of any photo directly in your browser — no software or sign-up required.

  • Keep aspect ratio: Automatically adjusts height when you change width (and vice versa) to avoid stretching.
  • Output format: Choose PNG, JPG or WebP depending on your needs.
  • Privacy: Your image is processed locally and never uploaded to a server.

Ideal for resizing photos for websites, social media, email attachments or printing.
