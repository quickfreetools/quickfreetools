Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reduce an image to 20KB?

Yes, the tool attempts to compress your image to 20KB or less. Very detailed images may require smaller dimensions or reduced quality.

Can I reduce photo size without losing quality?

Some compression may affect image quality. The tool attempts to preserve as much detail as possible while meeting your chosen file size.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens directly inside your browser.

Does this tool work on mobile devices?

Yes. The tool is designed for modern browsers on mobile phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.
